Research Areas

Core domains of expertise within the DeSy research group.

Dependability & Security

Development of intelligent techniques for dependability (availability, reliability, safety, security, integrity, and maintainability), security (confidentiality), and testing of information systems.

Analysis, design, implementation, and validation of dependable systems used in critical infrastructure such as energy, water, transport, and healthcare.

Cyber-Physical Systems (CPSs)

Development of abstractions, models, architectures, and tools for implementing reliable CPSs, including cloud–fog–edge systems built from potentially unsafe components.

Research on resilient CPSs capable of withstanding cyber and physical attacks, and modeling of heterogeneous systems and their associated logic.

Intelligent Systems

Analysis, design, implementation, and testing of intelligent real-time control and monitoring systems using artificial intelligence techniques.

Includes intelligent agents, fuzzy logic, machine learning, decision support systems, and deep neural networks.
